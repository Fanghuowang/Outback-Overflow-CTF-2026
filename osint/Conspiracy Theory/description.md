## Description

You are given a photograph from a conference event. Your task is to find the names of three specific attendees visible in the scene and use their first and last names to construct the flag.

Flag format: `OO{attendee_left_attendee_middle_attendee_right}`

Each attendee is `Firstname_Lastname`
